Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Wrestling Central News ("WCN", "we") aggregates and summarizes professional wrestling news and links to the original publishers. This policy explains what data is collected when you use this site.

Information stored on your device

We save your news filter selections, your cookie choice and the time of your last visit in your browser's local storage. This data never leaves your device.

Analytics

If you accept cookies, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand how the site is used (pages viewed, stories clicked, filters used, scroll depth, approximate location and device type). Google may set cookies such as _ga. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Advertising

We display ads served by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits. You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings or www.aboutads.info.

Your choices

You can decline cookies in the banner, or change your choice at any time with "Cookie settings" in the footer. Clearing your browser storage removes all locally saved data. Visitors in the EEA/UK may have additional rights under GDPR, and California residents under the CCPA, including access and deletion requests.

Third-party content

Headlines link to external publishers whose own privacy policies apply. Images belong to their respective owners and are displayed from the publishers' servers.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.
